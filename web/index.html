Harbiy-muhandislik o'yini · Godot 4 + C# A military engineering game · Godot 4 + C#

Sizni oddiy askar emas, harbiy muhandis-komandir etib tayinlaydilar. Konda material yig'asiz, mastserskayada uskuna ishlab chiqarasiz, front chizig'ida generator, telegraf va istehkom qurasiz. Har yopilgan vazifa kalendarni oldinga suradi — 1900-yildan 2050-yilgacha, o'nta davr.

You are not assigned as a soldier but as a military engineer-commander. You mine raw material, produce equipment in the workshop, and build generators, telegraph lines and fortifications at the front. Every task you close pushes the calendar forward — from 1900 to 2050, across ten eras.

01 G'oyaThe idea

Urush — kontekst,
maqsad emasWar is the context,
not the goal

AsosPremise

Jangchi emas, muhandisAn engineer, not a fighter

Ikkinchi vazifa uskunasiz umuman boshlanmaydi — HUD "KERAK: O'rnatish komplekti" deb turadi. Avval kon, keyin mastserskaya, keyin vazifa. O'yinning yuragi shu yerda.

The second task will not start without equipment — the HUD simply says "NEEDS: Installation kit". First the mine, then the workshop, then the task. That dependency is the heart of the game.

Signature mexanikaSignature mechanic

Vaqt mehnat bilan oqadiLabour moves the clock

Kalendar o'z-o'zidan emas, bajarilgan ish hisobiga siljiydi. Vazifa yopilganda ekran o'rtasida yangi yil chaqnaydi va butun davr almashadi: boshqa map, boshqa texnika, boshqa vazifa.

The calendar does not tick on its own; it advances on finished work. Close a task and the new year flashes across the screen, switching the whole era: new map, new hardware, new objectives.

JangCombat

Otishma bor, g'alaba emasFirefights, but no win by force

Dushmanlar sizni ko'radi va otadi, tank to'pi ishlaydi, texnika buziladi. Lekin missiya jang bilan emas — muhandislik vazifalari yopilgani bilan tugaydi.

Enemies spot you and shoot back, the tank cannon works, vehicles take damage. But a mission never ends in a firefight — it ends when the engineering tasks are closed.

01

RazvedkaRecon

Walk the map: where the mines, the workshop, the task points and the camp are.

02

Press E at a deposit — timber, iron, coal, stone. The MATERIAL panel on the left grows.

03

Ishlab chiqarishProduction

Mastserskayada buyurtma bering. Detal emas — sanoat ishlab chiqarishi: progress tugaguncha kutasiz.

Place an order at the workshop. Not part-by-part crafting — industrial output: you wait for the run to finish.

04

VazifaTask

Uskuna sumkada bo'lsa, vazifa ochiladi va bajarilganda sarflanadi.

With the kit in your satchel the task unlocks — and consumes the kit when it completes.

05

Vaqt sakraydiTime jumps

Uchala vazifa yopilgach bonus to'lanadi, kalendar siljiydi, keyingi davr ochiladi.

When all three close, the bonus is paid, the calendar moves and the next era opens.

Brifing — har missiyaning boshiThe briefing — how a mission opens

Har missiya brifing kartochkasi bilan boshlanadi: davr, tur, tavsif va uchta vazifa. Vazifa yonida talab turadi — KERAK: O'rnatish komplekti ×1 (0/1). Shu qator o'yinchiga nima qilish kerakligini bir qarashda aytadi: hali ishlab chiqarish qilinmagan.

Every mission opens with a briefing card: era, type, description and three tasks. Each task carries its requirement — NEEDS: Installation kit ×1 (0/1). That single line tells the player what is missing: production has not been run yet.

03 KampaniyaCampaign

O'n davr:

Har davr — o'z map'i, o'z vazifalari va o'z texnikasi. Missiyalar taxtada ochiq turadi: bajarilgani belgilanadi, keyingisi ko'rsatiladi.

Each era is its own map, its own tasks and its own hardware. Missions sit on the board: finished ones are stamped, the next one is flagged.

07 Texnik izohTechnical notes

Tizim talablariRequirements

Renderer ataylab gl_compatibility rejimida — zaif videokartalarda ham ishlaydi. Sinalgan: Apple M5, OpenGL 4.1 Metal. Diskda ~1 GB joy kerak.

The renderer deliberately runs in gl_compatibility mode so weak GPUs are still fine. Verified on Apple M5, OpenGL 4.1 Metal. Needs about 1 GB of disk space.

Saqlash fayliSave file

  • macOS — ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/ChronoShift/savegame.json
  • Windows — %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\ChronoShift\savegame.json

Toza boshlash uchun shu faylni o'chiring.

Delete that file to start clean.

Nimada qurilganBuilt with

Godot 4.7.1 (.NET) va C#. Butun kontent — missiya, qurol, texnika, retsept, modul — kodda emas, .tres ma'lumot fayllarida: yangi davr qo'shish uchun kod refaktori shart emas.

Godot 4.7.1 (.NET) and C#. All content — missions, weapons, vehicles, recipes, modules — lives in .tres data files rather than code, so a new era does not require a refactor.
